Tier 01 · Foundation

Blue Core Solutions

A division of Acqua Rinova Group (Pty) Ltd

Specialist water risk and hydrogeological consulting for the mining sector.

WRISOur product: the Water Risk Immune System. The framework, executable artefacts and training at the core of everything we do. See how it works

First-principles technical rigour on every engagement: field-based hydrogeological investigation combined with industry-standard numerical modelling, probabilistic analysis and data-driven reporting, from a rapid desktop review to a full site characterisation campaign to international finance and investor criteria.

Our practice areas

Five integrated technical practice areas address water risk holistically, from geological controls at depth to surface runoff and climate exposure at the catchment scale.

  1. Hydrogeology

    Subsurface characterisation, groundwater modelling, aquifer risk and contaminant transport in complex geological settings.

  2. Surface water & flood risk

    Rainfall-runoff analysis, hydraulic modelling (HEC-RAS 1D & 2D), IDF analysis, rainfall readiness plan development and flood risk mapping for mining operations.

  3. Mine water management

    Site-wide water balances (probabilistic & deterministic), dewatering strategy development, tactical dewatering & seepage management plans and water governance.

  4. Safety & risk studies

    Pit slope water pressure monitoring and risk assessments, underground inrush hazard assessments, tailings dam water management inputs and geotechnical review board support.

  5. Regulatory & advisory

    Environmental authorisation specialist inputs, feasibility study inputs (NI 43-101, JORC), independent technical reviews, audit and assurance support to mine operations.

Tier 02 · Operational enablement

Blue Harvest Projects

Industrial water mitigation, with the Roots & Rows design tool

Secure the water, then put it to work: mine water and surplus land turned into productive agriculture.

Blue Harvest secures and allocates water across the project lifecycle, from aquifer potential studies and water supply feasibility to abstraction licensing, adaptive allocation and post-closure water strategy. Where the evidence supports it, we turn the water a mine must manage and the land it must rehabilitate into a viable agribusiness.

  • Water supply & aquifer studies

    Aquifer potential, water supply feasibility, infrastructure planning and regional aquifer sustainability.

  • Licensing & allocation

    Abstraction licensing strategy, adaptive water allocation planning and shared water governance modelling.

  • Roots & Rows, a Blue Harvest solution

    Our automated design tool turns a site’s location, climate and water data into crop options, water budgets and an outline layout, so a discovery briefing ends with a picture of what the site could grow.

  • Closure & community value

    Dewatering treated as a productive input rather than a discharge liability, supporting closure planning, local jobs and food production, structured for development finance.
