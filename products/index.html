01

Data-driven AI decision tool

Rinova Nexus Decision Analytics

One reading of the catchment that operators, agronomists, lenders and regulators can all act on.

In development. Launching 2027.

The water data already exists. It sits in borehole telemetry, abstraction logs, lab results, rainfall records, spreadsheets and messaging groups that nobody reads together. Rinova Nexus brings it into one place and uses AI to turn it into decisions: what to expect, where the risk sits and what to do next.

  • Scenario forecasting

    Site water balances and farm water budgets at 80%, 60% and 40% of normal rainfall, plus a high-heat case, updated as climate guidance changes.

  • Licence and allocation headroom

    Your position against water use licences, allocations and curtailment triggers, visible before the regulator or irrigation board sets it for you.

  • One shared dashboard

    Site, farm and catchment data in a single view, built into daily and monthly operating routines rather than read once a quarter.

  • Audit-ready evidence

    Water use, energy per cubic metre and outcomes recorded in a form that supports lender, buyer and ESG reporting.

Built for mine water managers, commercial farm operators, lenders and ESG teams.

02

Industrial water mitigation, with the Roots and Rows design tool

Blue Harvest

Mine water and surplus land, turned into productive agriculture.

Mines pump water they must manage and discharge, and hold land they must rehabilitate. Blue Harvest tests whether that water and land can carry a viable agribusiness, then designs it. Evidence comes first: suitability, water quality, salinity, storage, legal right, partner capability and market demand.

  • Site and water assessment

    Land suitability, water quality and volumes, climate modelling and crop water needs, tested before anything is built.

  • Roots and Rows design tool

    Our automated design tool turns a site’s location, climate and water data into crop options, water budgets and an outline layout, so a discovery briefing ends with a picture of what the site could grow.

  • Closure and ESG value

    Dewatering treated as a productive input rather than a discharge liability, supporting rehabilitation, closure planning and community commitments.

  • Fundable structure

    Designed to suit development finance and enterprise and supplier development funding, with local jobs and food production built in.

03

Governance framework and team training

Water Risk Immune System

Teach the organisation to catch water risk early and respond before it becomes a crisis.

Most water failures are governance failures. The data was there; nobody owned the decision. The Water Risk Immune System is a framework for governing water as a regenerative system, and a training programme that teaches teams to think that way, so preparedness becomes a habit rather than a project.

  1. Detect

    Monitoring discipline and early-warning triggers, so signals are seen rather than buried in a logbook.

  2. Respond

    Curtailment and escalation protocols agreed in advance, while trust still exists, not written in a drought.

  3. Learn

    Every event feeds back into the water balance, licence strategy and design assumptions.

  4. Regenerate

    Decisions that restore the catchment, through recharge, reuse and riparian health, not only protect the site.

Training. Practical workshops for leadership, operations, environmental and community teams, grounded in structured change management so the new habits stick after the facilitators leave.

Built for boards, executive committees, operations and environmental teams, and irrigation boards.
